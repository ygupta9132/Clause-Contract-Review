MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding records the shared intentions of Arcadia Civic Trust and
Lowfield Community College regarding the joint use of the Arcadia Hall.

BACKGROUND

Arcadia Civic Trust maintains the Arcadia Hall on Market Street. Lowfield Community College
runs evening courses and requires teaching space two evenings each week during term time.

THE ARRANGEMENT

The Trust will make the main hall and the two side rooms available to the College on Tuesday
and Thursday evenings between 6pm and 9pm during term time.

The College will be responsible for setting out and clearing away its own equipment, and for
leaving the rooms in the condition in which it found them.

The Trust will provide heating, lighting and access to the kitchen.

Each party will nominate a single point of contact and the two will meet once a term to review
how the arrangement is working.

NOT A CONTRACT

This Memorandum is a statement of intent. It is not intended to create legal relations and
neither party intends it to be enforceable in law. Nothing in it obliges either party to enter
into any binding agreement.

Either party may bring the arrangement to an end at any time by telling the other.
