LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT FOR COMMERCIAL PREMISES

THIS LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT is executed at Pune on 5 May 2026 BETWEEN Mr. Suresh
Deshpande, aged 58 years, residing at Bungalow 14, Koregaon Park, Pune 411001, hereinafter
referred to as "the Licensor", AND Brightline Analytics LLP, a limited liability partnership
registered under the LLP Act 2008 having its office at Level 3, Amar Tech Park, Balewadi,
Pune 411045, hereinafter referred to as "the Licensee".

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of office premises admeasuring 4,200 square feet
on the second floor of Meridian House, Baner Road, Pune ("the Premises"), and the Licensee
has approached the Licensor for permission to occupy and use the Premises on leave and licence
basis.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. GRANT OF LICENCE

1.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee leave and licence to occupy and use the Premises for
the purpose of carrying on its business of software development and data analytics.

1.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates any tenancy, lease, sub-lease or any interest in the
Premises in favour of the Licensee.

2. PERIOD

2.1 This licence is granted for a period of sixty (60) months commencing from 1 June 2026 and
expiring on 31 May 2031.

2.2 The parties may, by mutual written agreement executed not less than three months before
expiry, extend this licence for a further period on such terms as may then be agreed.

3. LICENCE FEE AND DEPOSIT

3.1 The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of INR 5,46,000 (five lakh forty six thousand
rupees), payable in advance on or before the fifth day of each calendar month.

3.2 The licence fee shall escalate by five percent (5%) on each anniversary of the
commencement date.

3.3 The Licensee has paid an interest free refundable security deposit of INR 32,76,000, to be
refunded within thirty (30) days of the Licensee vacating the Premises, subject to deduction
of any amounts lawfully due.

3.4 Goods and services tax, where applicable, shall be payable by the Licensee in addition to
the licence fee.

4. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE

4.1 The Licensee shall pay for electricity, water and internet consumed in the Premises on
actuals.

4.2 The Licensor shall be responsible for structural repairs. Day to day maintenance of the
interiors shall be the responsibility of the Licensee.

5. USE OF PREMISES

5.1 The Licensee shall not use the Premises for any unlawful or hazardous purpose, nor store
inflammable material.

5.2 The Licensee shall not make any structural alteration without the prior written consent of
the Licensor.

6. LOCK-IN AND TERMINATION

6.1 There shall be a lock-in period of thirty-six (36) months from the commencement date,
during which neither party may terminate this Agreement save for material breach.

6.2 After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six (6)
months prior written notice to the other.

6.3 The Licensor may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the licence fee remains
unpaid for two consecutive months.

7. INDEMNITY

7.1 The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all claims arising from the Licensee's
use of the Premises.

8. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION

8.1 Stamp duty and registration charges in respect of this Agreement shall be borne equally by
the parties.

9. JURISDICTION

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Pune shall have
exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising hereunder.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the day and year first above written.
