NON-EXCLUSIVE RESELLER AGREEMENT

1 PARTIES

1.1 This Agreement is made between Halvard Instruments AS, organisation number 918 442 771,
of Nedre Slottsgate 8, 0157 Oslo, Norway ("Halvard"), and Pacific Lab Supplies Pty Ltd, ACN
632 118 904, of Unit 7, 14 Boundary Road, Carole Park QLD 4300, Australia ("Reseller").

2 APPOINTMENT

2.1 Halvard appoints the Reseller as a non-exclusive reseller of the Products in the
Territory, and the Reseller accepts that appointment.

2.2 "Products" means the laboratory instruments listed in Schedule A, as amended by Halvard
from time to time on sixty days notice.

2.3 "Territory" means Australia and New Zealand.

2.4 The Reseller has no authority to bind Halvard or to make any representation or warranty
about the Products beyond those in Halvard's published documentation.

3 DURATION

3.1 This Agreement takes effect on 1 October 2026 and shall continue for an initial term of
two (2) years.

3.2 Following the initial term, this Agreement shall continue automatically from year to year
unless terminated by either party under clause 9.

4 ORDERS AND PRICING

4.1 The Reseller shall purchase Products at the distributor prices in Schedule B, which
represent a discount of thirty-two percent (32%) from Halvard's published list prices.

4.2 Halvard may amend Schedule B on ninety (90) days written notice. Orders accepted before
the effective date of any amendment shall be fulfilled at the previous price.

4.3 The Reseller shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.
Payments shall be made in Norwegian kroner by electronic transfer without set-off or
deduction.

4.4 Halvard may suspend deliveries while any undisputed invoice remains overdue by more than
fifteen (15) days.

5 MINIMUM PURCHASE

5.1 The Reseller shall purchase Products with an aggregate invoice value of not less than NOK
4,000,000 in each contract year.

5.2 Failure to meet the minimum in any contract year entitles Halvard to convert the
appointment to a referral arrangement on thirty (30) days notice.

6 MARKETING AND BRAND

6.1 The Reseller may use Halvard's trade marks solely to promote and resell the Products, in
accordance with Halvard's brand guidelines.

6.2 All goodwill arising from such use accrues to Halvard.

7 CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Neither party shall disclose to any third party, nor use for any purpose other than
performing this Agreement, any confidential information of the other party, including pricing,
customer lists, and technical specifications.

7.2 These obligations continue for three (3) years following termination of this Agreement.

8 WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

8.1 Halvard warrants that Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for
twenty-four (24) months from the date of shipment.

8.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, Halvard shall have no liability for indirect,
incidental or consequential losses, and Halvard's total liability in any contract year shall
not exceed the amounts invoiced to the Reseller in that year.

8.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits any liability which cannot lawfully be limited, including
under the Australian Consumer Law.

9 TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the initial term, or at the end of
any subsequent year, by giving not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days written notice.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other
commits a material breach which is incapable of remedy, or which it fails to remedy within
thirty (30) days of being required to do so.

9.3 On termination, the Reseller shall cease to hold itself out as a reseller of the Products
and shall return or destroy all marketing materials.

10 GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement is governed by Norwegian law.

10.2 The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the Oslo District Court.

10.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other,
such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.
