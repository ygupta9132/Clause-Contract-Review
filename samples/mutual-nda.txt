MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made between Harbourline Technologies Pvt Ltd, having its office at
22 Marine Drive, Mumbai 400020 ("Harbourline"), and Castellan Research GmbH, having its
office at Rosenthaler Strasse 40, 10178 Berlin ("Castellan"). Each may be referred to as a
"Party" and together as the "Parties".

RECITALS

The Parties wish to explore a potential commercial relationship and, for that purpose, each
may disclose confidential information to the other. This Agreement sets out how that
information must be treated.

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the
"Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), in any form, including technical data, product
plans, customer lists, financial information and the existence of discussions between the
Parties.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information which: (a) is or becomes publicly
available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's
possession before disclosure; or (c) is independently developed by the Recipient without use
of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

2.1 The Recipient shall hold the Discloser's Confidential Information in strict confidence,
shall not disclose it to any third party, and shall use it solely for the purpose of
evaluating the potential commercial relationship.

2.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to those of its employees and
professional advisers who need to know it for that purpose, provided they are bound by
confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

2.3 The obligations in this clause 2 shall survive for five (5) years from the date of
disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information.

3. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

3.1 If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose any
Confidential Information, it shall, where lawfully able to do so, give the Discloser prompt
written notice so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement commences on 15 February 2026 and shall continue in force for two (2)
years, unless terminated earlier under clause 5.

4.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one (1) year unless
either Party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than thirty (30) days before the
end of the then current term.

5. TERMINATION

5.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving thirty (30) days written
notice to the other Party.

5.2 Termination of this Agreement does not release either Party from the confidentiality
obligations in clause 2, which continue for the period stated in clause 2.3.

6. NO LICENCE, NO WARRANTY

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence or other right in respect of
the Discloser's Confidential Information or intellectual property.

6.2 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party makes any warranty as to
its accuracy or completeness.

7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
Singapore, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Singapore.

8. GENERAL

8.1 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an
original.

8.2 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the written consent of the other Party.
