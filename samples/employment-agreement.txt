EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made on 12 January 2026 between Kestrel Software Labs Private Limited,
having its registered office at 7th Floor, Prestige Atrium, Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025
("the Company"), and Ms. Ananya Rao, residing at 212 Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad 500033
("the Employee").

1. APPOINTMENT

1.1 The Company appoints the Employee as Senior Software Engineer, reporting to the Head of
Engineering.

1.2 The Employee shall devote her full working time and attention to the business of the
Company and shall not engage in any other employment or consultancy without the prior written
consent of the Company.

2. COMMENCEMENT AND PROBATION

2.1 The Employee's employment commences on 2 February 2026 and continues until terminated in
accordance with clause 7.

2.2 The first six (6) months of employment are a probationary period, during which either
party may terminate on seven (7) days written notice.

3. REMUNERATION

3.1 The Company shall pay the Employee an annual cost to company of INR 32,00,000 (thirty two
lakh rupees), payable in equal monthly instalments on or before the last working day of each
calendar month.

3.2 The Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of fixed pay, at the
discretion of the Company and subject to the Employee being in service and not under notice on
the date of payment.

3.3 All payments are subject to deduction of tax at source and statutory contributions.

4. HOURS AND LEAVE

4.1 Normal working hours are 9.5 hours per day, five days a week, with flexibility as required
by the role.

4.2 The Employee is entitled to 24 days of paid leave per calendar year, in addition to public
holidays notified by the Company.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Employee shall not, during employment or at any time after it ends, disclose to any
person any confidential information concerning the business, clients, finances or technology of
the Company.

5.2 The obligation in clause 5.1 continues without limit of time in respect of trade secrets,
and for three (3) years following the end of employment in respect of other confidential
information.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of employment vests in the
Company absolutely.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 After probation, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving ninety (90) days
written notice, or payment of salary in lieu of notice.

7.2 The Company may terminate the Employee's employment with immediate effect and without
notice in the event of gross misconduct, wilful breach of this Agreement, or conviction of an
offence involving moral turpitude.

8. NON-SOLICITATION

8.1 For twelve (12) months after the end of employment, the Employee shall not solicit any
client of the Company with whom she had material dealings in the final twelve months of
employment.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have
exclusive jurisdiction.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

10.1 This Agreement supersedes all prior discussions and constitutes the entire agreement
between the parties in relation to the Employee's employment.
