ENGAGEMENT FOR BRAND DESIGN SERVICES

Thank you for agreeing to work with us. This letter sets out what we have agreed. It is
intended to be legally binding. Please sign and return a copy.

What you will do

You will design a new visual identity for Harbourfield Advisory, including a primary logo, a
secondary mark, a typographic system, and a short brand guidelines document. You will also
provide two rounds of revisions on each deliverable.

When

You will begin on 27 April 2026. We have agreed a delivery date of 31 July 2026 for the final
guidelines document, with the first concepts presented no later than 22 May 2026.

What we will pay

Our fee for the work is EUR 18,500 plus VAT, payable in three instalments: EUR 6,000 on
commencement, EUR 6,000 on acceptance of the first concepts, and the balance of EUR 6,500 on
delivery of the final guidelines. We will settle each invoice within fourteen days of
receiving it.

Expenses above EUR 250 must be agreed with us in writing before you incur them.

Ownership

On payment in full, all intellectual property in the delivered designs transfers to
Harbourfield Advisory. You may show the work in your portfolio.

Keeping things to yourself

Both of us will keep confidential anything learned about the other's business during this
engagement, and will not share it with anyone else without permission. This continues to apply
after the work ends.

Ending the engagement

Either of us may end this engagement by giving the other thirty days written notice. If we
end it early, we will pay for work properly completed up to that date. If you end it early,
you will return any fees paid for work not yet delivered.

Law

This letter is governed by the laws of Ireland and the courts of Ireland will deal with any
dispute.

Please confirm your agreement by signing below.
