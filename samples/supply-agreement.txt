AGREEMENT FOR THE SUPPLY OF GOODS

ARTICLE I - PARTIES

This Agreement for the Supply of Goods is made between CASTELLO PACKAGING SPA, a company
organised under the laws of Italy, having its registered office at Via Mecenate 76, 20138
Milano, Italy (the "Supplier"), and VERDANT FOODS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in
India, having its registered office at Plot 19, MIDC Industrial Area, Nashik 422010, India
(the "Buyer").

ARTICLE II - SUBJECT MATTER

2.1 The Supplier shall manufacture and supply, and the Buyer shall purchase, corrugated
packaging in the specifications set out in Annexure 1.

2.2 Quantities shall be as stated in purchase orders issued by the Buyer from time to time and
accepted by the Supplier.

ARTICLE III - TERM

3.1 This Agreement enters into force on 1 July 2026 and remains in force for a period of three
(3) years, expiring on 30 June 2029.

3.2 Expiry shall not affect any purchase order accepted before the expiry date, which shall be
completed in accordance with its terms.

ARTICLE IV - PRICES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Prices are as set out in Annexure 2 and are fixed for the first twelve (12) months.

4.2 Thereafter prices may be revised once per contract year by written agreement, and failing
agreement either party may terminate under Article VIII.

4.3 Payment shall be made by irrevocable letter of credit within sixty (60) days of the bill
of lading date.

4.4 Title to the goods passes to the Buyer on full payment. Risk passes on delivery at the
named port in accordance with Incoterms 2020 CIF Nhava Sheva.

ARTICLE V - QUALITY AND INSPECTION

5.1 The Supplier warrants that goods conform to the specifications and are free from defects
in material and workmanship for twelve (12) months from delivery.

5.2 The Buyer shall inspect goods within fifteen (15) days of arrival and notify the Supplier
of any visible defect within that period.

ARTICLE VI - CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential the commercial terms of this Agreement and any
technical information disclosed by the other party, and shall not disclose the same to any
third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

6.2 This obligation survives for a period of five (5) years from the date of expiry or
termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE VII - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 The Supplier shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of
production, loss of profit or loss of contracts.

7.2 The Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall in no event exceed the value
of the goods in respect of which the claim arises.

ARTICLE VIII - TERMINATION

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one hundred and eighty (180) days
written notice to the other party.

8.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the
other party becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or commits a material breach which is not
remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

ARTICLE IX - FORCE MAJEURE

9.1 Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by an event beyond its reasonable
control, provided it notifies the other party within seven (7) days.

ARTICLE X - GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and
finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre
in accordance with its Rules, with the seat of arbitration in Singapore and proceedings in the
English language.
